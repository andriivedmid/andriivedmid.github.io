Wembdle ↗

A daily NBA guessing game inspired by Wordle. Read a player-season’s eight-category radar chart and work out who it is, or pick which of two stat lines is better. It also has an all-time ranking model across eras, which is a genuinely fun way to start a basketball argument.

Dining Wrapped ↗

Inspired by an existing Dining Wrapped site for Brown, I made my own with new features: a graph showing how eating habits changed over time, a calculator for how many swipes were wasted and what they cost, and a breakdown of trends by dining hall.
